Pillars

How Pulse reads community health

Environment

Air quality now; heat, smoke, water, and weather impacts later.

Disease & Outbreaks

WHO notices now; CDC and regional alerts later.

Healthcare

HRSA shortage areas first; capacity, wait-time, and spending signals later.

Well-Being

CDC PLACES first for activity, obesity, mental health, and sleep measures.

Population & Society

Census population estimates first for growth, aging, births, deaths, and migration.
